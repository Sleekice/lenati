The evidence ledger

Every publication has an identifier, publication date, source, topic, status, original summary and review timestamp. Proposed claims such as a fixed number of “heart folds” remain unverified until a defined anatomical structure and credible supporting measurements are supplied.

Scientific updates require editorial review before release; automated metadata checks alone cannot establish that a finding is true. See our research and versioning protocol ↗.

ABOUT LENATI

Science deserves wonder — and evidence.

Lenati is an interactive encyclopedia prototype bringing together anatomy, physics, mathematics, nature, and education.

Our mission

Make biological structures and their connections accessible through exploratory learning. Keep actual scientific relationships distinct from metaphor, mathematical similarity, and artistic visualization.

What is already real?

The legacy atlas contains bundled anatomical meshes. This new hub provides searchable structures, curated paths, 19 viewpoints, learning decks, and an API-ready data model. Not every organ is currently an interactive 3D mesh.

Scientific provenance

Each catalog entry and learning card should link to the relevant source. Model-derived or illustrative experiences are labeled accordingly. Clinical simulation is not approved for diagnosis.

Credits and sources

Medical science references are provided below. The original atlas retains its own notices and licensing. Verify mesh redistribution requirements before publishing the bundled atlas publicly.

Prototype status: This is an educational demonstration. The static GitHub Pages deployment exposes JSON data files; a production API, clinical validation, secure authentication, verified female/age-specific anatomy, and accurate fluid simulation are future work.
